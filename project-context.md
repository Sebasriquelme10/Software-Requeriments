# ITESO Events Calendar — Project Context

## Project Overview

ITESO Events Calendar is an interactive digital platform for the ITESO community.

The platform is intended to help students, professors, staff, and campus collectives discover, organize, promote, and manage institutional, academic, cultural, and sports events within the university campus.

Examples of events may include:

- Academic conferences and workshops
- Student collective meetings and USI activities
- Cultural exhibitions, film screenings, and festivals
- Sports tournaments and physical health activations (CEFSI)
- Professional development and Alumni networking sessions
- Institutional ceremonies and campus-wide celebrations

## Business Opportunity

A large variety of events take place daily at ITESO, but information is often fragmented across physical posters, email blasts, and disparate social media channels.

At the same time, students and staff frequently miss out on activities due to a lack of a centralized, real-time schedule.

ITESO Events Calendar explores the opportunity to:

- Centralize campus activity management into a single trusted platform.
- Increase student engagement and participation across campus activities.
- Improve visibility for student collectives, sports teams, and cultural groups.
- Streamline event promotion for university departments (e.g., DCI, Arte y Cultura, CEFSI).
- Facilitate venue capacity planning and attendance tracking.
- Foster a vibrant, connected, and informed university community.

## Target Community

Potential users include:

- Students (USI, student collectives, sports capitanías)
- Professors and Academic Program Coordinators
- ITESO Staff and Department Heads (Arte y Cultura, CEFSI, DCI, DSI)
- Alumni / Egresados ITESO

Other stakeholders may be identified as the project evolves.

## Current Functional Epics

The project currently considers the following functional areas.

### 1. Account & Profile Management
Functionality related to user verification via ITESO single sign-on, profile setup, academic department classification, and interest preferences.

### 2. Notifications & Alerts
Functionality that keeps users informed via automated push reminders, urgent schedule change alerts, waitlist status updates, and capacity warnings.

### 3. Administration
Functionality that supports event creation, attendee limits, multi-university guest management, custom permission roles, and administrative controls.

### 4. Registration & Attendance
Functionality related to calendar syncing, QR code check-ins, registration cancellations, group reservations, and attendance verification.

### 5. Event Discovery & Search
Functionality that helps users search, filter, and discover campus events by date, category, department, or real-time popularity trends.

### 6. Community & Social Engagement
Functionality related to social sharing, public Q&A sections on event pages, and student interaction surrounding campus activities.

### 7. Analytics & Reporting
Functionality that provides real-time attendance statistics, participation metrics across departments, and data export capabilities (e.g., CSV).

### 8. Feedback & Reviews
Functionality that enables registered attendees to submit post-event ratings and actionable quality feedback to event organizers.

### 9. Venue & Map Integration
Functionality related to interactive campus maps, pinned event venues, and location-based filtering for nearby campus activities.
